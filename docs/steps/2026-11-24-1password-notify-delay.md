**1Password notify delay: approving at the Mac sends nothing (2026-11-24, branch `1password-notify-delay` from `sill-0.6.2`).** The notification went out the moment a prompt appeared, so a prompt approved at the Mac still buzzed the iPhone.

- `PromptNotifier` sends only for a prompt that has been open 20 s (`delay`). 1Password closes a prompt after 60 s, and Sill cannot change that, so about 40 s are left to approve from the iPhone. Noah chose 20 s over 30 s and 45 s.
- A prompt that closed after 59 s or more (`timedOutAge`) timed out unanswered: seen once a second, a timeout reads 59 to 61 s and an approval near the end 58 s at most. The next prompt within 5 minutes (`missedWindow`) is sent at once. A prompt that closed sooner was approved, which ends that, and so does any approval among prompts that close together. While the Mac is locked the notifier forgets what it saw, so a prompt closing during a lock is no miss.
- The 15 s gap between notifications is gone: with the delay, a later prompt always waits longer than it, and one notification still covers every prompt open when it is sent.
- Verified on Mac Studio and the iPhone: one prompt notified 21 s after it opened; a prompt left to time out, then a second one notified about 1 s after it opened, approved from the iPhone. `Tests/checks/catalog-windows`: 160 checks and 87 of 87 mutants.
