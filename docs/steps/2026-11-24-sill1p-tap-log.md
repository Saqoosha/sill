**Sill 1P tap log: each tap's fate on the iPhone (2026-11-24, branch `sill1p-tap-log` from `sill-0.6.2`).** After a 1Password notification, the iPhone's first tap on the prompt once never reached the Mac, and a second tap worked. The Mac's log could not tell whether the touch reached Sill 1P.

- `TapLog` writes each touch's down, up and system cancel by surface (stream or trackpad), each tap and long press sent or dropped with its reason (a silent stroke, or outside the picture with the picture's rect and the video size), what the session link did with each button event (sent, kept behind a hand-over, or dropped with no connection), each video size change, and which branch the approval link took. It goes to the unified log and to `Documents/tap.log`, because a launch from a notification has no console attached.
- Pull it with `xcrun devicectl device copy from --device S --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier sh.saqoo.sill-1p --source Documents --destination <dir>`.
- Verified on the iPhone Air, office Wi-Fi, under a cover window on the screen saver level: link opened, video 3840×2160 then 400×326 within 0.35 s, touch at 4.4 s, tap sent, Authorize pressed on the Mac 0.15 s later. The loss did not reproduce. When it was seen, the iPhone was away from the office on Tailscale.
- `tap.log` moves to `tap.1.log` past 1 MB. It holds coordinates only, never typed text. A failed open never replaces the file, and write failures go to the unified log.
